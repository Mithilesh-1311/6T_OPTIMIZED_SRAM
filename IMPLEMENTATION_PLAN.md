## Scientific blockers and limits
No independent final-test evidence exists, so credible≥90% final predictive performance remains unestablished. Current curves and the historical netlist/setup record support the configured mappings, positive-terminal power convention and corrected SNM construction; raw exports cannot independently certify temperature/corner/bias/topology or simulator convergence. Full ADE states/decks/logs would strengthen provenance. No new simulations or Cadence execution are claimed. Browser automation was not available in the session; manual visual smoke checking remains.
