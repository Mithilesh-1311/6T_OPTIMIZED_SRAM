# SAMS additional simulations required

The existing evidence supports the functioning application and exploratory regressions. It does not establish ≥90% predictive performance on independent final cases. This is an unexecuted acquisition plan; no new Cadence runs are claimed.

## Freeze before acquiring evidence
Keep the current metric definitions, model candidates, primary MAE and within-tolerance target frozen. Reserve final conditions before inspecting their labels. Group both modes/states and every related export for a condition together. Do model selection on development conditions only; evaluate the selected pipeline once on the reserved final conditions. Do not repeatedly retune against final results. Preserve ADE states, full generated netlists, Spectre logs and source settings with each run. Repeated solver runs at the same deterministic condition test convergence, not new independent configurations.

## Reserved final conditions
Reserve all temperatures **40 and 70 C** at every listed voltage and both candidate modes/states as the untouched final condition set: 20 active cases, 20 retention cases, 10 hold-SNM cases and 10 read-SNM cases. These exact conditions are absent from the existing evidence. Use temperatures27/55/85 C for development; group paired actions together. Preserve final labels separately until the implementation/model selection is frozen. Report the number of independent condition groups as well as case counts; paired actions do not double the independent temperature/voltage coverage.

Interior voltages and temperatures test interpolation instead of only opposite corners. Additional extreme voltage/temperature cases improve coverage of interactions and boundary behavior. Matched controls distinguish idle leakage from full workload energy. Exported supply/control voltages replace filename-only assumptions. Logs separate electrical failure from simulator nonconvergence and incomplete exports.

This campaign addresses interpolation under the same nominal model section, geometry, loads and schedules. It still cannot establish process-corner, mismatch/yield, shortest safe retention voltage, earliest wake read, physical-area, layout or silicon claims. Those require separately scoped experiments; they are not prerequisites for the present schematic software deliverable.
